FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a campus problem? +

Click "Report an Issue", select the problem category, location and priority, then submit your complaint.

Can I track my complaint? +

Yes. Every complaint receives a unique complaint ID that can be used to track its progress.

Can I upload a photo? +

Yes. You will be able to attach photos to help maintenance staff understand the problem.

Will I know when my issue is resolved? +

Yes. Your complaint status will be updated as the maintenance team works on it.
